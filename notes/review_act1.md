# Yodgor — Act I rewrite (v3): review sheet

25 before → after samples from `src/layer/70_act1.js`, picked so you can judge the whole job in ten minutes. Items 1–12 are choice menus; 13–25 are lines. "Before" is the v2 text, "after" is v3. English is given once, only where it helps.

Every option is now a line the hero says or a short action. Option colours were already removed by the engine; now the tags are gone too. The Hollowness cost is never printed. Where an option eats Ravshan himself, the words say so.

## Uzbek I'd like you to check (less certain)
- **pahr** for a night watch («tunning ikkinchi pahri», «har pahrda boqadi»). It is old and bookish. If it sounds too archaic, «posbonlik» or just «tun yarmi» would work.
- **oshiq-moshiq** for the singing hinge of the blue gate (clue 3). The other choices are «lo'la» or «sharnir». I chose the folk word.
- **«issiq-sovuq» o'yini** for the "warmer/colder" game in the memoryNear bark.
- **Alla-yo alla** as the opening of Sado's lullaby (ALLA_1–3, the Rabat scene and m_alla). The traditional opening is «Alla-yo alla» or «Alla, bolam, alla». I made it consistent across all five places.
- **«Jonim shundoq ham qil ustida turibdi — uning qo'lida»** for "She already holds my life on a string".
- **tentakvoy** (Sado teasing Ravshan: "Sing, silly"). Before it was «ahmoqcha». Is the tone right for a child?
- **Yettov** for "the Seven" (founders) inside dialogue. Glossary-style «Yettilar ahdnomasi» is kept for the Treaty itself.
- **bitta katta to'lov** for Shirin's "one great payment". It was «buyuk to'lov». I kept «Buyuk ushr» for the Great Tithe itself, so the euphemism and the real name differ, as they do in English.
- **kishan / kishanlamoq** for "chain" (the Sleeper chained by a song) instead of «zanjirband qilmoq».
- **Kimoshdi savdosi / dallol** for auction and auctioneer.
- **Xudo yorlaqasin** in Mehri's mouth. She is an old, pious widow, so it is deliberate. Everyone else avoids religious words.

# Regions — review sheet: locals and finds (89b_world_folk.js), RU / UZ

Six regions that looked empty got one local each (a short exchange, then a last word on later visits) and two finds (lore up close, some with coins). No flags, no Hollowness, nothing about Sado. RU and UZ were written from the meaning; the English is only the source of meaning.

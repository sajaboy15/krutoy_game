# Regions — review sheet: locals, finds and moments (89b_world_folk.js), RU / UZ

Six regions that looked empty got one local each (a short exchange, then a last word on later visits), two finds (lore up close, some with coins) and one moment on the way (once). No flags, no Hollowness, nothing that hints at who Sado is. RU and UZ were written from the meaning; the English is only the source of meaning.

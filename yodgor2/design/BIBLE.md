# YODGOR — Story Bible v2 (source language: English)

Working reference for writing, translation and implementation. Engine: the «Kyok Bori» action roguelite (gameplay unchanged). Languages: EN (source), RU, UZ (Latin script, ASCII apostrophe: o', g').

## 1. Logline
In a city built on the back of a sleeping water spirit, a memory-tithe collector is bound by a song that will hollow him out by the new moon unless he finds the stolen relics of the city's founders. A girl's voice in his head helps him. She doesn't know who she is. Neither does he.

## 2. Themes and tone
- Memory as currency: the poor sell their best moments to survive; the rich pay their tithe with other people's lives.
- Forgetting as violence; remembering as a burden; a story given is shared, a memory taken is lost.
- Love too large for a tumar.
- Tone: dark, adult, melancholic, restrained. Poetic but always clear: one image per line, short sentences, concrete senses (salt water, apricots, lamp oil, wet clay, tumar wax). Never melodrama, never modern slang. Dry humour lives in Farrukh and, sometimes, in Sado.
- Style rules for lines: ≤ 220 characters per dialog page; names and terms from the glossary only; no exposition dumps — reveal through people who want something.

## 3. World
- **Yodgor** ("keepsake, memory"): an oasis city on the back of a sleeping spirit the size of a mountain range. The city calls him *the Div* or *the Sleeper*. Water seeps from cracks in his skin: warm, a little salty; bitter when he is hungry.
- **Districts**: the Ark (on his shoulders: palaces of the Divan), the Bazaar and the Cloister of the Singers (along the spine), the Tail (the curled tail: the poor), the Rabat (caravanserai outside the walls), the Karez (water tunnels inside his body; the buried streets of Old Yodgor), the Dream.
- **Memory craft**: *Take* (a memory is sung into a tumar, a triangular amulet; the owner loses it whole), *Taste* (live a memory for minutes; each taste wears it), *Plant* (sing a memory into someone; they believe it is theirs), *Burn* (a memory flares into power — the only magic — and is gone forever), *Feed* (Singers sing tumars to the Sleeper in the Well).
- **The Tithe**: once a year, on the Night of Payment, every adult gives the Singers one strong memory. The rich pay with bought memories. The poor sell their own until they become **Hollow** — people who walk, work and answer, but without themselves. Hollow are drawn to full tumars like the hungry to bread.
- **Forty Days**: a foreigner may stay forty days without tithe; then pays or leaves.
- **Why it breaks now**: tasted memories wear thin and the Sleeper does not eat them. The Divan's market resells the same worn tumars; for years the tithe has been made of them. The Sleeper starves: the water turns bitter, the ground shakes, people dream other people's dreams.
- **Hidden history**: the Sleeper is **Obi-Hayot** ("water of life"), a water spirit. People told him tales; he raised water from the sand (the first bakhshi were his storytellers). Seven caravan-bashi learned that stolen memory lulls him deeper than tales. They chained him with a song and poured the memories of captives into him. In the **Year of Sand** he woke mad and erased Old Yodgor in one night. There was no treaty. The heirs of the Seven became the Divan; the bakhshi became the Singers of the Well; songs of the water spirit were banned and his name forgotten. The founders' own memories are kept as relics — **the founders' tumars** — and they hold the chaining song.
- **Twenty years ago — the Small Awakening**: the Sleeper turned in his sleep; half of the Tail slid into the sand. The Singers doubled the Tail's tithe.

## 4. Cast
- **Ravshan** — the hero, a *yodchi* (tithe collector), 32. Apprenticed to Rustam-aka at 12. At 12 he sold "the most precious thing he had" to close his family's debt; he does not know what it was. Named after the hero of the dastan *Ravshan* (Turab notes the irony). Tired, precise, dry; decent under a professional crust; speaks little; counts things when nervous. Childhood name: **Toychoq** ("little foal"), used only at home.
- **Sado** — the companion, a girl's voice in his head that wakes when Shirin's Pledge Song binds him. She does not know her name; Ravshan calls her *Sado* ("echo, a voice"). Quick, curious, playful, sometimes uncannily old. Loves apricots, rain, the dutar, counting stars. Hates the dark and the smell of tumar wax. Acts as his operator: warns, identifies enemies, notices hidden memories, reads stones with him, argues with his choices. **Truth (revealed in Act III):** she is the part of his memory of his sister Oydin, aged eight, that did not fit into the tumar he sold at twelve — love too large for the amulet stayed behind as a scar, and the Pledge Song woke it.
- **Rustam-aka** — Ravshan's mentor. Found Hollow at the vault door. Hollow people repeat habits: he walks the old tithe route and sits in the same chaikhanas. Twenty years ago he wrote Ravshan's little sister into the debt book ("the tithe was counted twice") and never told Ravshan. On the Night of Payment he gave Oydin the vault key himself and asked her to empty him.
- **Ustoz Shirin** — Elder of the Singers of the Well, 60s. Lost her daughter in the Small Awakening. Keeps the Sleeper asleep at any price; plans the **Great Tithe** (sing the whole Tail empty in one night). Binds Ravshan with the **Pledge Song** (garov qo'shig'i). Tragic, terrifying in conviction; her voice is beautiful.
- **Akhror Tilla-bek** — head of the House of the Humo and of the Divan, 50s. Cultured, charming, collects rare memories ("a thousand childhoods in his cellar"). Wants the founders' song to chain the Sleeper and sell memory along the whole Silk Road. Believes the shadow of the Humo bird has fallen on him.
- **Oydin, She Who Remembers** — leader of *Those Who Remember*, 28. Ravshan's younger sister. Taken as a debtor at eight; sung by the Singers every Night of Payment; escaped last winter. Keeps one memory of her brother in a tumar sewn under her collarbone and tastes it once a year. Stole the founders' tumars. Wants to wake the Sleeper so that everyone remembers everything — and will drown the city for it. Hums the lullaby *Alla*.
- **Nargiza** — Singers' apprentice, 19, hears the Sleeper (the Order calls it a sickness). Shirin's favourite. Meets Ravshan at the Cloister; may come down into the Karez; can become the voice that sings the tale.
- **Alp** — huge Hollow ex-caravan guard in the Tail, protector of orphans. Does not know his name. His tumars (Swallowed Streets) reveal he was Tilla-bek's captain who sold people for extraction.
- **Farrukh** — memory smuggler, disowned son of the House of Saffron; lives on stolen happy memories.
- **Turab** — the mute bakhshi; his voice was taken for refusing to sing the tithe; it is sold at auction. Knows the old tales for Obi-Hayot.
- **Mehri** — widow in the Tail; her only strong memory is her son, drowned in a karez.
- **Davron** — caravan-bashi at the Rabat hiding three children from the tithe.
- **Gulnora** — a Tail girl whose memory of first love was sold and planted into a merchant's daughter in the Ark (*The Stranger's Wedding*).
- **Bahodir, the Gilded Hound** — Tilla-bek's champion; his loyalty is a planted memory.
- **Obi-Hayot** — the water spirit; mad with three hundred years of other people's lives.
- **Ak Bars** — the white guardian of the spine, chained by a song.

## 5. Mechanics ↔ story
- **Hollowness (Void) 0–100**, HUD bar under health. The Pledge Song sings him empty a little at a time.
  - Rises: each death (Pilgrim Easy +2 / Normal +4 / Hard +6; Oath +5; Hollow Road: death ends the run), tasting memories (+8), paying with his own memory (+15), burning a memory with the *Burn* skill (+3 per use), certain cruel choices.
  - Falls: recovering Ravshan's own lost memories hidden in each land (−6 each, also tell his past and seed clues), Sado's lullaby at a chaikhana (−4, once per chaikhana, needs trust), Mehri's gift (−8), the sister memory in Act III (−30).
  - Effects: ≥50 Sado's voice crackles and some personal dialogue options lock; ≥75 colour drains from the screen and NPCs notice; 100 = the Hollow ending at once.
- **Sado's trust** (hidden): mercy and honesty raise it, cruelty and betrayal lower it. Unlocks her lullaby, some hints and the final options.
- **Faction standing**: Singers, Divan, Those Who Remember, the Tail, the Rabat. Shapes later scenes and the epilogue.
- **Lost memories of Ravshan** (collectible tumars, one hidden per land): first snow, the gate with the singing hinge, apricots over the wall, the lullaby… each lowers Hollowness and carries a clue about the girl he forgot.
- **Modes**:
  1. *Pilgrim* — choose Easy / Normal / Hard. Death returns you to the start of the land (Sado pulls you back; the song takes a memory).
  2. *Yodchi's Oath* — fixed hard difficulty. Death returns you to the start of the land with the same layout; all tilla is dropped where you fell as a *lost tumar* and can be recovered once.
  3. *Hollow Road* — permadeath: death ends the run.
- **Sado as operator**: non-blocking barks (subtitles) during play; a small wisp of light near Ravshan that brightens in the dark, dims as Hollowness grows, and drifts toward hidden memories.
- **People at exits**: each land ends with a real group of people standing near the gate; walking up to them starts the scene.

## 6. Structure (prologue + three acts, ~32 stages, target 8 h)
PROLOGUE — *The Night of Payment*
- L  Tail at night — Rustam sends Ravshan ahead and leaves "for the vault". Hollow follow his full satchel. **Exit: the widow Mehri** (take by force / accept a counterfeit / pay with your own memory / write her down as paid). Tremor.
- B  **The Singers' Vault — The Glutton** (Hollow fused around spilled relic memories). After: Rustam Hollow at the door; Shirin and her Singers; the Pledge Song; darkness; **Sado wakes** ("Is it dark? Please don't let it be dark.").
ACT I — *The City*
- R  Chaikhana of the Two Plane Trees — Hollow Rustam (taste 1), mute Turab, Farrukh.
- L  Tail, the morning after — Sado remembers a blue gate that fell twenty years ago. **Exit: a collapsed house, Alp and the neighbours digging** (help / give water and tilla / collect the debt).
- L  Rabat and the Salt Road — **Exit: Davron's caravan, three children in waterskins, a Singers' inspector coming** (lead them out / report / take the bribe / bluff the inspector). Sado teaches him the lullaby for the children.
- B  **Tower of Silence — Yalmog'iz Kampir**. After: living Hollow in chains with the Well's seal (carry the chain to the Tail / keep the seal / stay silent).
- R  Chaikhana — Rustam (taste 2: the girl in the cellar, name Oydin), Turab's mime, Farrukh's habit.
- L  Memory Bazaar — Sado freezes at a girl with two braids. **Exit: the auction of Turab's voice** (buy / steal / leave). Gulnora asks for help.
- L  The Ark, gardens of the Divan — **Exit: Tilla-bek's reception and the Stranger's Wedding** (Tilla-bek's offer; Gulnora's love: give it back / leave it / blackmail).
- B  **The Gilded Hound — Bahodir**. After: his loyalty is planted (show him / finish him / spare him).
- R  Chaikhana — Rustam (taste 3: the Great Tithe), Turab speaks if freed, Farrukh offers a founders' tumar (taste: there was no treaty).
- L  Cloister of the Singers — **Exit: Nargiza, Shirin's sermon to the Tail elders**; Nargiza asks to come down.
- B  **The Silent Choir** — seven Hollow singers whose soundless song drains memory (hits add Hollowness). After: the Well opens; the water turns bitter; the descent.
ACT II — *Under the Skin*
- R  Smugglers' den in the upper Karez — messengers from all three factions; Rustam (taste 4: the key).
- L  The Karez — darkness; Sado's fear ("I was afraid of the dark when I was little." "When were you little?" "…I don't know."); her light. **Exit: Those Who Remember** — a masked woman tells him to turn back and hums *Alla* as she leaves.
- L  The Swallowed Streets — **Exit: Alp's tumars in a Divan cache** (give them back / destroy them / let him choose).
- B  **Albasty, Mother of the Karez**.
- R  The Last Lamp (underground chaikhana).
- L  Old Yodgor — **Exit: Oydin unmasked**: "Hello, Toychoq." The sister reveal. "You're not alone in there. Who is looking at me?" (join / take the tumars / let her go / show Rustam's memory / taste her tumar).
- L  The Founders' Well — a girl's face beside his in the black water.
- B  **The Seven** — the founders' shades.
- R  Chaikhana.
- L  The Spine of the Sleeper.
- B  **Ak Bars** — break the chain / tighten it for the Divan / kill him. The name **Obi-Hayot**. Nargiza's question (believe the voice / go back to Shirin / silence it).
- R  Chaikhana on the Spine — Turab's tales (needs his voice and the name).
- L  The Heart — **Exit: the plan of the strongest faction begins**; the Sleeper stirs; the city above cracks; Nargiza's song (or Oydin's path) takes Ravshan into the dream.
ACT III — *The Dream*
- L  The Dream of the City — 300 years of memories as streets.
- L  The Drowned Wedding — a two-hundred-year-old wedding loops forever; Mehri's son if his memory was taken by force.
- L  The Year of Sand — the night Old Yodgor was erased, replayed.
- R  The dream chaikhana — everyone as they are remembered; Rustam's last clear words ("Toychoq…").
- L  Your House — the house that smells of bread and grief; Sado knows every board. **The reveal**: the sold tumar is cracked — a piece is missing. Sado is the piece. Choice about Sado: *remember her* (merge) / *keep her* / *give her to Oydin* / *let her become the tale* (only if the tale is possible).
- B  **Ajdaho — the Nightmare of the Sleeper**.
- Finale with Obi-Hayot → ending.

## 7. Endings
City: **New Tithe** (Singers), **The Divan's Chain**, **Awakening** (Oydin), **The Tale** (hidden: Nargiza believes; the name and the truth known; Turab's tales; the sister remembered or Sado given as the tale; Hollowness < 60), **Hollow** (defeat at 100).
Personal: Sado remembered / kept / given to Oydin / became the tale. Epilogue slides are assembled from flags: the city, the Tail, Shirin, Tilla-bek, Oydin, Sado, Nargiza, Alp, Farrukh, Turab, Mehri, Davron and the children, Bahodir.

## 10. Added characters (author's request, Nov 2026) — important lore participants
- **Sherzod** — 24, a scribe of the Divan's memory ledgers at the Bazaar; quick, ironic, brave in words, careful in deeds. He is gay. In Yodgor love that "does not serve the family line" is treated by the Singers as a debt of the soul and may be taken as tithe. Two years ago his love for a caravan guard, **Jasur**, was sung out of him on the Night of Payment. He knows something is missing — he keeps a page of Jasur's handwriting he cannot remember the meaning of. Written with full respect: his orientation is part of who he is, never a joke and never his only trait.
- **Bobokhon-aka** — 58, Sherzod's father, a respected Tail potter and elder of his mahalla. It was he who wrote his son's love into the debt book, believing he was saving him from shame and the Singers' anger. He loves his son and cannot look at him. His arc is a father's guilt and the question of whether a stolen love can be returned — and whether the son will forgive the father.
- **Role in the lore:** Sherzod's ledgers record where taken memories go (a key clue for the Bazaar and the Divan arc: the same worn tumars resold again and again). Bobokhon knows the old potters' wax recipes for tumars (the Potters' Quarter, the wax workshop).
- **Side story «Отцовская десятина» / "A Father's Tithe" / «Ota ushri»** (Tail, Potters' Quarter, Bazaar, Ark cellar): Bobokhon asks Ravshan, as a yodchi, to find "a tumar he wrote down two years ago" without saying whose. Investigation through Taste: the debt book, Sherzod's page, the tumar resold in Tilla-bek's cellar. Choices (grey): return the love to Sherzod (he will remember Jasur — who has since married and left, and who he cannot have back; and he will learn who took it from him), keep the truth from him and give the tumar to the father, sell the tumar to the Divan for the ledger proof Sherzod needs, or let Sherzod decide without knowing his father's part. Consequences: Sherzod's help with the Divan ledgers (Divan arc), Bobokhon's wax recipes (crafting), the father–son relationship, epilogue slide.
